## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an immersive, theater-first digital atmosphere tailored for communal movie discovery, streaming curation, and collective decision-making. The personality balances the electric anticipation of stepping into a dark cinema auditorium with the refined ergonomics of modern, high-tier entertainment platforms.

### Aesthetic Foundation
- **Modern Cinematic Dark Canvas:** Pure deep carbon foundations create contrast for rich cover art, vivid video stills, and glowing interactive triggers.
- **Atmospheric Luminosity:** Subtle, diffuse colored ambient glows (golden butter/warm amber paired with electric violet) mirror projector spill and cinematic lighting rigs.
- **Tactile Ergonomics:** Surfaces invite physical interaction with polished, frosted glass membranes, micro-reactive press states, and balanced geometric roundedness.
- **Clarity & Urgency:** Streamlined metadata presentation prevents analysis paralysis when users decide what to watch.

## Colors

The palette pairs a cavernous, low-light backdrop with sharp luminescent accents that pull focus to primary calls to action, user ratings, and streaming availability.

### Core Chromatic Roles
- **Primary (`#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24`):** Cinema Amber / Golden Popcorn. Drives critical calls to action, active tab highlights, star ratings, and watch party confirmations.
- **Secondary (`#8B5CF6`):** Electric Violet. Anchors secondary features, discovery feeds, matchmaking cues, and premium curation markers.
- **Tertiary (`#6366F1`):** Indigo Glow. Provides ambient depth, subtle interactive hover gradients, and secondary accents.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0B0E14`):** Deep Graphite Void. Serves as the base backdrop to maintain OLED depth and theatrical focus.

### Surface Tiers & Overlays
- **Surface Level 0 (Base Canvas):** `#0B0E14`
- **Surface Level 1 (Card Bases, Drawers):** `#161B26`
- **Surface Level 2 (Elevated Sheets, Menus, Modals):** `#212936`
- **Surface Level 3 (Active Chips, High-Focus Rows):** `#2D3748`
- **Border Ghost (Stroke Subtlety):** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`
- **Border Highlight (Active State):** `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.40)`

### Text Hierarchies
- **Text High-Contrast:** `#F8FAFC` (Glacier White, 95% opacity)
- **Text Medium:** `#94A3B8` (Slate Neutral, 70% opacity)
- **Text Muted:** `#64748B` (Subtle metadata, runtimes, timestamps, 50% opacity)

### Brazilian Age Rating System (Classificação Indicativa)
Standardized, non-negotiable regulatory badge values:
- **Livre (L):** Solid `#00B050` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **10 Anos:** Solid `#0090FF` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **12 Anos:** Solid `#FFC000` with `#000000` text.
- **14 Anos:** Solid `#FF6600` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **16 Anos:** Solid `#FF0000` with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **18 Anos:** Solid `#000000` with `#FFFFFF` text and a subtle `#DC2626` outline.

### Streaming Provider Signature Badges
- **Netflix:** Brand Red `#E50914` on `#000000`
- **Prime Video:** Prime Blue `#00A8E1` on `#0E1A24`

- **Apple TV+:** Monochrome Silver `#E5E7EB` on `#1F2937`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy uses **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve a balance between geometric modernity and organic approachability.

### Editorial Guidelines
- **Hero & Feature Headings:** Use tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) on large display tiers to create a cinematic poster-like impact.
- **Synopsis and Metadata:** Keep body copy at regular weight (`400`) in `#94A3B8` on dark backgrounds to minimize eye strain and preserve readability.
- **Badges and Indicators:** Labels, streaming pills, and regulatory classifications leverage `label-sm` or `label-md` with `font-weight: 700` and uppercase tracking for legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around a content-first responsive grid structured to display high-density media posters and sweeping horizontal rails.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Grid Rules
- **Mobile (`< 640px`):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter-sm: 1rem`. Movie rails extend edge-to-edge with negative horizontal margins, allowing posters to overflow with right-side scroll peeking.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`):** 8-column layout with `margin-md: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Two-column synopsis splits; carousels switch to snapping row structures.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12-column layout capped at a maximum width of `1440px` with `margin-lg: 4rem` and `gutter-lg: 2rem`. Dedicated side navigation bar (80px collapsed / 240px expanded) with multi-row media grids.

### Spatial Rhythm
Inner-card content padding uses `space-md` (`1rem`). Inter-section gaps between media carousels scale to `space-2xl` (`3rem`) to maintain clear thematic separation.

## Elevation & Depth

This system treats surfaces as layers of translucent smoked glass floating in an auditorium space, relying on tinted backdrops and soft specular boundaries rather than heavy drop shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Floor):** Base `#0B0E14` void. Non-elevated, flat canvas.
- **Level 1 (Docked Containers & Poster Cards):** Solid `#161B26` with a hairline stroke: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`. No shadow at rest.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns):** `#212936` elevated with an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 16px -2px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.12)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, and Quick-Action Sheets):** Smoked Glass. `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`, surface fill `rgba(22, 27, 38, 0.85)`, bordered by `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

### Ambient Glow Technique
Interactive focal elements (such as "Iniciar Sessão" or "Match!") cast a localized, soft radial gradient blur behind the element using `#F59E0B` or `#8B5CF6` at 15% opacity with a `32px` blur radius.

## Shapes

The interface uses comfortable, organic geometry that balances fluid streaming media with structured interfaces.

### Curvature Tokens
- **Poster & Content Cards:** `rounded-2xl` (`1rem` / `16px`). Creates a smooth framed edge for movie artwork without clipping critical metadata.
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors):** `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / `12px`). Balances thumb target hit areas with a refined, tactile aesthetic.
- **Metadata Badges & Streaming Chips:** `rounded-md` (`0.375rem` / `6px`). Keeps small tags crisp and space-efficient.
- **Floating Action Buttons & Avatars:** Fully rounded pill (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary ("Bora Assistir"):** Background `#F59E0B` transitioning subtly to `#FBBF24`, text `#0B0E14` font-weight `700`. Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Radius: `rounded-xl`. Active state triggers a subtle scale-down (`transform: scale(0.97)`) for tactile feedback.
- **Secondary (Watch Trailer, Details):** Background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, text `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`. On hover, fills with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.14)`.
- **Party/Social Highlight:** Background `#8B5CF6` with white text, accented by an ambient violet diffuse shadow.

### Cards (Media & Movie Posters)
- Aspect ratio of `2:3` for standard movie posters; `16:9` for landscape previews.
- Enclosed in `rounded-2xl` with `overflow: hidden`.
- Base border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Hover State:** Poster zooms smoothly (`scale(1.05)`) over 300ms cubic-bezier transition, while a bottom gradient overlay rises from `rgba(11, 14, 20, 0.95)` to reveal quick action controls (watchlist toggle, match heart, and trailer play).

### Streaming Service Badges
- Compact pills (`h-6`, `px-2`) featuring the official platform logo paired with optional micro-labels.
- Outlined with subtle platform-tinted translucent borders (e.g., `rgba(229, 9, 20, 0.3)` for Netflix).

### Brazilian Rating Chips (Classificação Indicativa)
- Dimensions: Square `22px x 22px` with `rounded-md`.
- Centered typography using `label-sm` with bold weight.
- Applied strictly according to the regulatory background color tokens defined in the color specification.

### Input Fields
- Dark well background: `#161B26` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Placeholder color: `#64748B`. Focused state transitions border to `#F59E0B` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)`.

### Chips & Genre Filters
- Height `36px`, horizontal padding `1rem`, `rounded-full`.
- Default: `#161B26` with border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` and text `#94A3B8`.
- Selected: Background `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`, border `1px solid #F59E0B`, text `#FBBF24`.
